Ringwell press kit

Ringwell is a medication alarm that actually rings: set it by talking, and it keeps ringing until you confirm the dose.

About Ringwell

Ringwell is a free medication alarm for iPhone, made by Pelmavio, a one-person studio. Instead of filling in a form, you say the whole plan in one sentence, in English or Chinese: the medication, the dose, before or after meals, the times and how many days. Ringwell fills in the details, reads them back, and saves nothing until you confirm. At the set time it rings as a real system alarm, built on Apple’s AlarmKit, not a notification that is easy to miss. Pressing Stop only silences it: a dose counts only when you mark it as done, and until then Ringwell rings again every 5 minutes, up to 11 more times. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and medications and dose history are kept on the phone.

Three key messages

1. It rings, even on Silent.
A real alarm, not a notification you can miss (when Ringwell is allowed to set alarms).
2. Stop isn’t “taken.”
Only your confirmation counts, and it rings again until then, every 5 minutes, up to 11 more times.
3. Just say it, in English or Chinese.
If you can talk, you can set it up. Ringwell reads the plan back and saves only after you confirm.

Features

  • Set it by voice. Say the medication, dose, timing, times and how many days in one sentence. Or type it in by hand.
  • Hear it back, then confirm. Fix anything that was misheard. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
  • A real alarm. System alarms that show on the lock screen. If you don’t respond, it rings again every 5 minutes, up to 11 more times by default.
  • Stop is not done. Silencing an alarm never counts as taking a dose. Mark it as done, or skip it.
  • Today at a glance. Each dose shows its status: pending, done, skipped or not confirmed.
  • Every dose on the record. One circle per day, by week or month. Doses that weren’t confirmed are listed so you can fill them in later.
  • Know before you run out. Enter how many you have left; a note on Today tells you when it’s time to refill.
  • Easy to read and tap. Adjustable text size, high contrast, big buttons and Dark Mode.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no analytics. Erase all data from Settings at any time.
