FAQ

My alarm did not ring. What should I do?

Check that Ringwell is allowed to schedule alarms (Settings > Ringwell), turn off Low Power Mode, make sure no Focus mode silences it, and update iOS to the latest version. Then open Ringwell once so it can reschedule. Alarm behavior depends on your device and system settings.

How do I turn off reminders or delete my data?

To pause one medication, switch it off in its detail page. To delete one, remove it in the app. To erase everything, go to Settings > Privacy and data > “Erase all data”, which also cancels all alarms. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone, in the app’s private storage. Ringwell has no account and no server that receives your medication data. Because we do not hold it, we cannot recover deleted data. iOS backups may include app data according to your system settings.

How do I contact you?

Email support@pelmavio.com. Please do not include sensitive health details in your email.

Is Ringwell medical advice?

No. Ringwell only reminds and records. It does not diagnose, treat, or advise on medications or doses. Follow your doctor or pharmacist, and seek care if you feel unwell.
