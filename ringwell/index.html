Example sentence: “Two tablets of daily meds after meals, morning and evening, for five days.”

“Two tablets of daily meds after meals, morning and evening, for five days.”

One sentence. The whole plan.

Say the medication, the dose, the timing, the times and how long to take it, all together. Ringwell fills in the form for you.

Hear it back. Then confirm.

Ringwell reads the plan back to you. Fix anything that is wrong. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Then a real alarm rings.

Not a quiet notification: a system alarm, set with Apple’s AlarmKit (iOS 26 or later). Until you mark the dose as done, it rings again every 5 minutes, up to 11 more times.

Stop only silences it. It does not mark the dose as done.

Stop is not done
Silencing an alarm never counts as taking a dose. Only you can mark it done.
